Technical Whitepaper & Scope

About ProductGuard & AI Methodology

Engineering a computer vision framework for visual counterfeit detection via transfer learning and high-dimensional packaging embedding comparison.

Computer Vision Pipeline Architecture

Counterfeit products frequently present micro-discrepancies in packaging typography, brand insignia alignment, aspect ratio, color palettes, and finish texture. ProductGuard applies deep convolutional feature representation to capture these visual characteristics.

Mathematical Formulation

1. Pre-processing: The uploaded image is decoded, verified against JPEG/PNG file headers, transformed to standard RGB color space, and rescaled using bilinear interpolation to $224 \times 224 \times 3$.

2. Feature Space Mapping: The input tensor passes through the truncated MobileNetV2 backbone (pre-trained on ImageNet-1K). The global average pooling layer produces a raw vector $\mathbf{f} \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$.

3. $L_2$ Vector Normalization: $$\mathbf{e} = \frac{\mathbf{f}}{\|\mathbf{f}\|_2} = \frac{\mathbf{f}}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{1280} f_i^2}}$$

4. Cosine Similarity Computation: Given the query embedding $\mathbf{e}_q$ and candidate verified reference embedding $\mathbf{e}_r$, the cosine similarity equals their inner dot product: $$S(\mathbf{e}_q, \mathbf{e}_r) = \mathbf{e}_q \cdot \mathbf{e}_r = \sum_{i=1}^{1280} e_{q,i} \cdot e_{r,i}$$

Decision Threshold Calibration

Assessment Status Cosine Score Technical Interpretation Recommended Action
High Similarity ≥ 82% Strong visual alignment with verified manufacturer packaging standards. Visual packaging verification passed.
Moderate Similarity 65% – 81.9% General structural resemblance detected, but significant variations exist in print, fonts, or lighting. Secondary inspection of batch numbers, seals, and holographic tags required.
Low Similarity < 65% Substantial divergence from manufacturer packaging templates. High suspicion of counterfeit. Flagged as suspicious. Avoid purchasing or dispensing.
Insufficient Data < 40% / None No eligible verified reference products available in the specified category. Admin must ingest genuine manufacturer samples.
Scientific Disclaimer: Visual Similarity vs. Authenticity

ProductGuard operates strictly as a visual packaging verification system. It is designed to assist consumers, customs officials, and retail audits by catching packaging discrepancies, misprints, font anomalies, and unauthorized replicas.

Important: Visual similarity is not an absolute guarantee of chemical, pharmacological, or electronic authenticity. Sophisticated counterfeits may reuse discarded genuine containers ("refilled goods"), and genuine products may exhibit slight color variations due to regional manufacturing batches or extreme lighting conditions. Always combine visual AI verification with physical batch checks.
